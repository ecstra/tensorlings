## Decision

The tensorlings curriculum is built from what Karpathy's two syllabi, Stanford CS336, CS231n and CS224n, MIT 6.7960, three textbooks and PyTorch's module list agree a deep learning expert must know. It covers data, models, losses, optimizers, systems and evaluation in 13 parts, about 300 exercises.

## Alternatives

Framing every module as a constrained MLP, taken from a chat the owner pasted. It covered less than half of what the sources agree on and is wrong for attention, gates, flows, generative objectives and reinforcement learning. The owner called it biased.

## Consequences

The course is large and gets built in phases the owner reviews. Every new part is checked against the survey in the research doc before it is written.
