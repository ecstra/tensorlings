## Decision

Each exercise has three hints. The first says where to look, the second gives the idea, the third gives the answer. Pressing `h` shows the first. The next one opens only after the learner saves and the checks run again. The owner picked this.

## Alternatives

One hint with the answer in it, which the first version had, gave the task away. Opening the next level on every press of `h` lost, since learners who can click freely skip straight to the answer.

## Consequences

Every exercise needs three hints, and the app remembers how many are open until the learner moves to another exercise.
