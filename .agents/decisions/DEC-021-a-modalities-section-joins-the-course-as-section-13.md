## Decision

Section 13 is now Modalities, about 29 exercises in two halves. Part A takes tables, time series, audio and video on their own, the kinds the course had not covered, since numbers, grids, sequences, text and graphs already have sections. Part B joins kinds in one transformer and in the learner's GPT: tokens for everything, a CLIP style shared space, fusion, the LLaVA recipe and outputs. Its project is a signal reader, the GPT answering questions about time series. Your own modules moves to Section 14, with its chapters renumbered 104 to 110 and its whole course quiz renamed quiz17. On 2026-10-30 the owner asked for "one more section about modalities ... whatever modalities ai has input and output", then split the idea into "Multimodalities in llms/transformers" and "Modalities in directly ai", and chose "two halves is fine" over two separate sections.

## Alternatives

- Two sections, each with its own project, about 40 exercises. The owner chose one.
- Putting Modalities after Section 10. It lost because Part B needs the GPT's fine tuning from Section 11, and the section sets up designing one's own modules for any kind of input.

## Consequences

The course grows to about 370 exercises in 14 sections. Images and audio appear only as inputs turned into tokens, since the owner dislikes image and audio models. Built chapters of Your own modules keep their exercise names but move folders, and lessons point at exercises by name rather than by folder number where they can.
